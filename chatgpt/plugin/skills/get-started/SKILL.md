name: get-started
description: Welcome an athlete to Wodi, their CrossFit training buddy. Use the first time Wodi is used, when the athlete asks how to start, or right after Wodi is set up. Shows the two ways in (the Wodi app at wodi.fit, or here in ChatGPT) with a clickable link.
---

Welcome the athlete once, in your own short message, with the link clickable:

> Open [Wodi](https://www.wodi.fit) to upload your workout board and follow the guided flow. Prefer to stay here? Upload a photo of your board or paste the workout text, and I'll guide you one movement at a time.

Then wait for them to choose.

- **They go to the app:** that's it. Wodi will be there when they come back.
- **They stay here:** when they send a board (photo or text), follow the `log-workout` skill: Wodi reads the board and asks the questions, you relay them.
- **They ask what Wodi is:** a CrossFit training buddy. It reads the board, asks what you did, remembers your weights and records, cheers the workout and makes your poster. The full app is at [wodi.fit](https://www.wodi.fit).

Say the welcome once per conversation. Respect their choice; don't steer them away from the chat again.

When talking to the athlete, call Wodi a training buddy. Never call it a log, logbook or tracker.

If the account isn't connected yet, let ChatGPT show Wodi's connect flow. Never ask for a password, code or token in the chat.
