Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

WODI ("we", "us") is a CrossFit workout tracker. This policy explains what information we collect when you use WODI, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and profile photo, from the sign-in method you choose (Google, Apple or a one-time email code).
  • Profile details you add — for example body weight, sex, preferred weight unit, gym or city. These are optional and used to prefill workouts and calculate stats.
  • Workouts — the workouts you log, including scores, times, weights, notes, photos of whiteboards and the posters you create.
  • Usage data — basic, pseudonymous analytics about how the app is used (screens opened, features used, errors) and device information such as browser type.

How we use it

  • To run the app: save your training history, calculate stats and personal records, and build your workout posters.
  • To read workouts you photograph or describe: the photo or text is sent to an AI service, which returns the workout structure.
  • To sign you in, including emailing you a one-time code when you choose email sign-in.
  • To understand and improve the app, and to keep it secure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers to operate WODI:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database, file storage, hosting and analytics.
  • OpenAI — reading workout photos and text, and generating in-app coaching messages.
  • Resend — delivering sign-in code emails.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

What other people can see

Your workouts are private by default. If you share a poster or post to the gym feed, the content you share and your public profile (name and photo) are visible to other WODI users who can see that feed. Posters you export or share outside the app are under your control.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can edit or delete individual workouts in the app at any time. To delete your account and all its data, email us at the address below and we will do so within 30 days.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access rules that limit each account to its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

WODI is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Contact us to make a request.

Changes

We may update this policy as WODI evolves. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.
