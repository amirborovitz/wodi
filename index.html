WODI

Every workout deserves a poster. The CrossFit app that turns your training into something worth sharing.

Snap the whiteboard. Tell WODI how it went. Get a poster of the session in seconds, and at the end of every week, month and season, a Wrapped-style recap of everything you lifted, ran and survived.

Your training, made to show off

  • A poster for every WOD. Pick a look, stamp your mood, save it or share it.
  • Your Wrapped, all year round. Weekly, monthly and seasonal recaps: total reps, total weight moved, your top movement, your engine, new bests, and the training persona you earned.
  • A feed for your crew. Follow the people you train with, cheer their workouts, post a selfie with the board.
  • PRs that feel like PRs. New personal bests on lifts and benchmark WODs get their own moment.
  • Your week at a glance. See which parts of your body you trained and what's still waiting.

Logging that takes seconds

  • Snap the board. WODI reads AMRAPs, EMOMs, For Time, strength sets, chippers and partner WODs from a photo.
  • Just chat. "Got 6 rounds plus 10, 60 kg on the cleans." WODI logs it.
  • Remembers your weights. See what you loaded last time before you touch the bar.
  • A training buddy that knows your history. Ask WODI anything about your training.

WODI runs on iPhone, Android and desktop, right in the browser. Add it to your home screen and it works like an app.
